About NPIT

Education built with industry, for industry

Since 2011 we've partnered with employers to design programs that reflect the skills actually in demand — not the ones that were in demand a decade ago. Every syllabus is reviewed twice a year with our industry advisory board.

  • Small cohorts. Maximum 20 students per class, so instructors know your name and your goals.

  • Real projects. You graduate with a portfolio built on briefs from actual partner companies.

  • Career support for life. CV reviews, mock interviews and job referrals — available long after you graduate.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can't find what you need? Talk to an advisor.

Do I need previous experience to enrol?
No. Most of our programs are beginner-friendly and start from the fundamentals. For intermediate and advanced tracks we'll check your background during the advisor call and recommend the right starting point.
How long does a typical program take?
Between 14 and 26 weeks depending on the track. Full-time students usually study 30–35 hours per week; part-time evening cohorts run at around 15 hours per week.
Is there financial support available?
Yes. We offer monthly payment plans with no interest, a limited number of partial scholarships for career changers, and employer-sponsored invoicing for students whose companies are funding their training.
Are classes online, on campus, or both?
Both. Every program runs as a live online cohort, and our campus offers in-person sessions for Development, Data and Design tracks. You can switch between the two formats mid-program if your circumstances change.
What happens after I graduate?
You keep lifetime access to career support: CV and portfolio reviews, mock interviews, and referrals to our 200+ hiring partners. You also join the NPIT alumni network, which runs regular hiring events and workshops.
Will I receive a recognised certificate?
Yes. All programs lead to an NPIT certificate mapped to national occupational standards. Several tracks also prepare you for external vendor certifications such as AWS, CompTIA Security+ and Microsoft PL-300.
